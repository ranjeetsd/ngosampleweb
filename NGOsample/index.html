Who We Are

A Little Kindness Can Change a Lot.

ShreeIndoreNgo is a community-focused nonprofit initiative created to support people, expand opportunities, and encourage positive change in Indore. Through local participation and collaborative efforts, we aim to make education more accessible, support families in need, empower women, and inspire responsible environmental practices.

Our Mission

To encourage inclusive growth by supporting education, essential needs, and community-led development.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate society where every individual has the opportunity to learn, grow, and live with dignity.

Our Values

Compassion, integrity, inclusion, transparency, and collective responsibility.
